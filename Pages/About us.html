Our Vision, Purpose & Plan

Why we started and what we hope to achieve.

Our Vision

We imagine a community where people are better prepared for floods, understand the risks, and can make safer decisions during heavy rainfall.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to raise awareness about flooding in Taungoo and explore how an underground discharge tunnel could help manage excess rainwater.

Our Plan

We plan to research local flood risks, investigate possible solutions, create an educational website, and share our findings with the community.

How We Plan to Make a Difference

Our project journey, from research to presentation.

01

Research the Problem

Learn how heavy rainfall, rising rivers, and drainage problems can cause flooding in Taungoo.

02

Explore Possible Solutions

Investigate underground discharge tunnels and learn about their possible benefits and limitations.

03

Build Our Website

Share flood causes, safety advice, and our proposed solution through a clear and accessible website.

04

Present Our Ideas

Present our research and explain how awareness, early warnings, and suitable infrastructure may help reduce flood risks.
